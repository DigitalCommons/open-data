The .coop interactive map provides a visual database of all active .coop websites. All .coop domain registrants are verified to ensure that the cooperative is guided by the Cooperative Principles and the values that they promote. Cooperatives are the original ethical business!

DotCoop provides this directory at no charge for all active .coop domains. DotCoop is dedicated to helping people identify and locate co-ops on the Internet and next door.

You can search in your area easily or find a cooperative you know by name in the search box. Find co-ops near you that are dedicated to the cooperative principles and are "Proud to be .coop!"

Can’t find your favorite co-op? Check with them to see if they have a .coop website yet and if they don’t, let them know that you were looking for them on [https://identity.coop/directory!](https://identity.coop/directory/)

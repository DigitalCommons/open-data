Welcome to the Cooperative World Map!

The Cooperative World Map (CWM) is an interactive and up-to-date digital map that aspires to unite and showcase all cooperative entities in the world, and is the forward facing part of the [Cooperative World Data Project.](https://worldmap.coop/) The CWM is a visual representation of all cooperative enterprises in the world who have voluntarily shared their data to be part of this accessible and open digital platform.

After a year of expansion in 2025, the International Year of Cooperatives, we are aiming for even more in 2026. This year we explore more ways to grow the data to include new cooperatives in new regions, while enhancing the visual appearance and functionality of the map.

This [Mykomap](https://digitalcommons.coop/mykomaps/) has been created by the [Digital Commons Co-operative](https://digitalcommons.coop/) using data published by [DotCooperation](https://identity.coop/) and many [international co-operative partners](https://worldmap.coop/our-data-partners/) across the world under the Open Data Commons Attribution Licence ([ODC-By v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).)
